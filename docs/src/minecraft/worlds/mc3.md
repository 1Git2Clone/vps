**Join at** `mc3.hu-tao.dev`
**Pack** [vanilla-plus-26.2.mrpack](https://pages.hu-tao.dev/hutao/vps/mc3/vanilla-plus-26.2.mrpack)
**Minecraft** 26.2

The biggest of the three: a new world built on the Vanilla+ modpack, which
keeps Minecraft's look and feel and fills the world with more of it. New
terrain and biomes, many more structures to find, new animals, cooking and
farming, storage, backpacks and fast travel.

**You need the pack to join**: the world has blocks, items and mobs a vanilla
client does not know.

**Reset on 2026-10-29.** Wilder Wild, Naturalist and Streams Reflowing left
the pack that day, and the world started over without them. The first world is
kept on the server, not deleted.

## What changes for everyone

- **The world itself**:
  - [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/mod/terralith) and
    [Tectonic](https://modrinth.com/mod/tectonic): nearly 100 new biomes and
    much grander terrain.
- **More to find**: Dungeons and Taverns, Towns and Towers, Explorify and
  Repurposed Structures add villages, ships, dungeons, taverns and variants of
  vanilla structures; Sparse Structures spreads them out so they stay special.
  Explorer's Compass and Nature's Compass point you at structures and biomes.
- **Things to do**:
  - [Farmer's Delight](https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight-refabricated):
    cooking and farming.
  - [Tom's Simple Storage](https://modrinth.com/mod/toms-storage): a storage
    network with one terminal.
  - [Traveler's Backpack](https://modrinth.com/mod/travelersbackpack):
    upgradeable backpacks.
  - [Waystones](https://modrinth.com/mod/waystones): fast travel between
    waystones you find or build.
  - [Comforts](https://modrinth.com/mod/comforts): sleeping bags and hammocks.
- **Conveniences**:
  - FallingTree fells whole trees.
  - Ore Vein Miner mines a connected vein of ore at once: sneak while you
    break one with a pickaxe.
  - Dynamic Lights makes held torches light the area.
  - Clumps merges XP orbs.
  - Half the players can skip the night.
- **Distant terrain**: Voxy draws far-away land from what the server sends.
- **The server** runs a stack of performance mods (C2ME, Lithium, ScalableLux,
  ServerCore, Krypton, Alternate Current for redstone) and spark for profiling.

## What your pack adds

- **Performance**: Sodium and friends, Iris, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling,
  More Culling, Dynamic FPS, FastQuit.
- **Interface**:
  - Xaero's Minimap and World Map.
  - Jade (what you are looking at), AppleSkin, Shulker Box Tooltip, Mouse
    Tweaks. AppleSkin's saturation outline is gold: Overgrown Flowery GUI
    turns it the same blue as its hunger droplets, so the pack's own
    `appleskin-saturation` resource pack puts the gold back over it.
  - Ok Zoomer, Freecam, Chat Heads, Mod Menu.
- **Visuals and sound**:
  - Two shader packs: MiniatureXL, which is **on by default**, and
    Complementary Reimagined. Switch between them, or off, in _Video Settings_
    → _Shader Packs_.
  - Fresh Animations and its extensions, Not Enough Animations and Punchy for
    livelier mobs and players.
  - 3D Skin Layers, Falling Leaves, Particle Rain, AmbientSounds.
  - A set of vanilla-style resource packs (3D ladders, lanterns and flowers,
    connected glass and paths, dark GUI, lower fire and shield).

## Tips

- Shaders are heavy, and one is on from the start. If the game stutters, turn
  shaders off first; Complementary is the one to try on a strong GPU.
- Waystones are the way around a big world: activate every one you pass.
- The compasses are crafted; check a recipe in your inventory's recipe book.
