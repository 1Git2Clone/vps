**Join at** `mc2.hu-tao.dev`
**Pack** [gemstones-zombies.mrpack](https://pages.hu-tao.dev/hutao/vps/mc2/gemstones-zombies.mrpack)
**Minecraft** 1.21.1

A zombie survival world, on hard. Nights are dangerous, zombies hunt you by
sound, light and smell, and you have guns to answer with. It stays on
Minecraft 1.21.1 on purpose: that is the version these mods are made for.

**You need the pack to join.** The zombie mods add mobs, items and blocks your
game has to know about, so a vanilla or differently modded client is turned
away.

## What changes for everyone

- **Hordes** ([Mebahel's Zombie Horde](https://modrinth.com/mod/mebahels-zombie-horde)):
  some nights turn into survival events, with zombies that coordinate, share
  aggression and break through defences.
- **New zombies and mutants**
  ([Mutants and Zombies](https://modrinth.com/mod/mutants-and-zombies)):
  tougher variants than vanilla's.
- **Zombies that notice you**
  ([Zombie Awareness](https://modrinth.com/mod/zombie-awareness)): they track
  blood scent, sound and light, so a torch or a fight draws a crowd.
- **Guns** ([Vic's Point Blank](https://modrinth.com/mod/vics-point-blank)):
  craftable firearms, attachments and ammunition.
- **Bunkers** ([Underground Bunkers](https://modrinth.com/mod/underground-bunkers)):
  generated underground, worth finding. Server-only; nothing to install.
- **Trees fall**, **half the players can skip the night**, and **sound bounces
  and muffles**, as on world 1 (FallingTree, Server Sleep, Sound Physics
  Remastered). Chat stays unreportable.

## What your pack adds

Only what makes the game run well: Sodium, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling and
ModernFix. Everything else in the pack is something the world needs on your
side.

## Tips

- Light is a beacon here: think before you torch up a base in the open.
- If the game will not start or the server rejects a mod version, re-import the
  pack: the server's versions and the pack's always move together.
